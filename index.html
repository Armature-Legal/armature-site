Products

One foundation for the whole case.

Three products that work together or on their own: the case file and the office's day, the evidence and how it's shown in court, and the documents a case needs.

Case Management

From the first call to the courtroom.

Intake, the case file and the court calendar in one place. Everyone starts the day on a dashboard for their role, and the system flags what needs action before it becomes a problem.

  • Role dashboards: attorneys see their cases; supervisors see overall caseload; support staff see their open tasks
  • Triggers for what can't wait: an offender (or client) on release arrested on a new case, a trial with witnesses not notified, court with discovery not yet tendered or received
  • Work requests to investigators, trial technology and support staff, each with its own queue
  • Discovery tracked from request to tender or receipt; offers, witness contacts and availability on the case
  • Court calendar by courtroom, with in-court and in-office workspaces

Evidence Management and Presentation

Prepare the evidence. Show it in court.

Open case evidence from your digital evidence platform, plot it on a map and a timeline, and present it in court all from one app.

  • Built-in media viewer and editor for documents, photos, video, audio and reports
  • Map evidence in time and location, with movement synced to video
  • Link evidence side by side: photos, videos, and diagrams together
  • Present to a separate audience screen, with callouts and markings
  • Mark and admit evidence with customizable exhibit stickers

Document Builder

Court documents built from the case.

Jury instructions, motions, draft orders, subpoenas and other court documents, drafted from case information and checked against the official text. Every generated change is flagged for review.

  • Pattern jury instructions, searchable, with revision checks
  • Motions, draft orders, subpoenas and other filings from the case's parties and charges
  • Your own defaults, shared across your office or firm
  • Word export and courtroom slides, saved straight to the case
