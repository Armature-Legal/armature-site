Products

Case management

From the first call to the courtroom.

Felony review intake, the case file and the court calendar in one place. Each case opens to what matters at a glance, with defendants, witnesses, hearings and evidence a click away.

  • Intake call log, charging decisions and statistics
  • Case files with plea offers, witness contacts and officer availability
  • Court calendar by courtroom and call, in day, work week or month view
  • Search by court number, RD, defendant, agency, assignment or court date

Evidence

See the evidence without leaving the case.

Every item on the case in your evidence platform, grouped by folder. Open PDFs, photos, video and audio right in the case, step through them one by one, and share them with defense counsel without leaving the app.

  • Built-in viewer for documents, photos, video and audio
  • Share with defense counsel from the case
  • A permanent audit log of who viewed or downloaded what

Jury instructions

Instructions built from the official text.

Assemble Illinois Pattern Jury Instruction sets for criminal and civil trials. Names and bracketed choices are filled in, every automatic change is flagged for review, and the set exports ready to tender. Now online for invited users.

  • The full IPI library, searchable, with revision checks
  • Your own defaults, saved and applied to every new case
  • Word export (marked and clean sets) and courtroom slides
