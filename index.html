The suite

One foundation for the whole case.

Separate modules built on the same file: case management, document drafting and management, and evidence and jury preparation and presentation. Use them as one suite, or standalone tools alongside the systems you already use.

Case Management

From the first call to the courtroom.

Intake, the case file and the court calendar in one place, with each person's work laid out by priority.

  • Tasks for every role, ranked now, this week and later
  • Alerts for what can't wait, like a new arrest on a pending case
  • Court dates checked against the clerk's record
  • Discovery tracked from request to tender or receipt

Evidence and Presentation

Prepare the evidence. Show it in court.

Open the case's evidence from your digital evidence platform, build the trial file, and present it from the same app.

  • Clip video, redact documents and add Bates numbers
  • Map and timeline, with video and transcripts in sync
  • Present to the jury on its own screen, then export a verified jury package
  • Exhibits side by side, with callouts and markings

Document Builder

Court documents built from the case.

Charges, jury instructions, motions, orders and subpoenas, drafted from the case and checked against the official text.

  • Counts checked against the full statute, with lesser included offenses
  • Jury instructions suggested from the facts and the trial record
  • Every case cited is checked, linked and watched
  • Your office's clauses, signature blocks and defaults

As a suite

One sign-in and one case. Cite an exhibit in a draft, and a filed document's status lands back on the case file.

As a module

Each module connects to your evidence platform and court records on its own. Jury Instructions is also available by itself.
