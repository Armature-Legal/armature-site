Approach

Prepared is a structure,
not a feeling.

  1. 01

    The case, connected.

    Calls, charges, exhibits and decisions link to each other, so nothing is re-entered or reconstructed from memory.

  2. 02

    Work that holds up in court.

    Charges come from the statutes and instructions from the official text, with every change on the record.

  3. 03

    AI drafts. Lawyers decide.

    Every suggestion shows its source, and nothing reaches the court without review.

  4. 04

    Secure and on the record.

    Two-step sign-in, an audit trail on every action, and evidence verified when it leaves.
